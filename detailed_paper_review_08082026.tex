\section*{Scope and decision rule}
The revision uses \path{jmlr2025_beta_mix2_04202026.tex} as its base and
records accepted edits in
\path{jmlr2025_beta_mix2_08082026.tex}. Only localized corrections to
definitions, notation, grammar, typography, and plainly erroneous algebra or
algorithm statements were adopted. Changes that would introduce or strengthen
assumptions, rebuild a theorem, alter a rate, or replace a proof were deferred.
All accepted manuscript text is shown in red.

\textbf{Partially applied} means that a low-risk local component was corrected,
while the substantive remainder of the recommendation was deliberately left
unchanged. The two proposed assumption replacements concerning quantile
curvature and low-dimensional Lipschitz spatial fluctuation were not adopted.
